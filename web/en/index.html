    How it decides

    First it looks at what each title is made of. It takes it apart into pieces (genres, themes, who directed it, who is in it, what it's about) and searches the whole catalog for the titles that share the most pieces, and the hardest ones to share. Each group of pieces counts as much as its weight says. Then it listens to audiences: among the similar ones, it moves up what people who watched your title recommend, and what is best rated.

    How to read affinity

    The percentage says how alike two titles are, from 0 to 100. 100% would be the same title, so two different titles almost never go above 60%. Audience opinion doesn't change it: it only decides the order.

    Your search

    1. Under 25%

      Same style

      They share the kind of story and a theme or two. Good for discovering something different.

    2. 25–40%

      Very similar

      Same tone and themes, and often the same director or part of the cast.

    3. 40% or more

      Same family

      Sequels, spin-offs, or the same creator with the same cast.

    1. Takes each title apart

      With pandas, re and nltk it turns each card into tags: “crime”, “1980s”, “Vince Gilligan”, “Bryan Cranston”. From the synopsis it drops the stop words and keeps the stem of the rest (“fighting” → “fight”).

    2. Weighs what's rare

      Sharing something few titles have says far more than sharing something common: the same director is worth more than both being “Drama”. Tags no other title has are dropped, since they could never match.

    3. Compares in two ways

      It measures how alike their full vectors are with numpy (cosine similarity) and how many aspects they match, group by group. Affinity is the mean of the two: only what's alike in the rare and in the general scores high.

    4. Listens to audiences

      It moves up what the TMDb community recommends to people who watched your title (by up to 20%) and what's best rated, with the rating adjusted for votes as in IMDb's top 250. It precomputes everything and does it again on the 1st of every month.
